DATA & METHODS

What is in this explorer

This interface displays the four specific ATAC + H3K27ac + TE groups. The reserved opposite-pattern TE set is excluded. NBQX controls are 16-hour NBQX; the activated condition adds 1-hour BIC.

RNA statistics

The paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test uses four matched cultures per genotype and group. Exact two-sided sign permutations are computed from the absolute-difference ranks; zero differences are dropped and ties use average ranks. The displayed q-value is Benjamini–Hochberg adjusted across all eight prespecified genotype/group comparisons. The test is exploratory, not an interaction test.

Chromatin and Hi-C

Hi-C and ATAC tracks are pooled. Contacts, regions, and loops are not independent biological replicates. The sample-specific loop files are shown separately, with FDR meaning and source coordinate convention unverified. A shared visual scale supports exploration, not a replicated differential test. Locus examples are selected and may be biased.
